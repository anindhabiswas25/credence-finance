# ADR-0201 · Historical market data for calibration: source, licence and pinning

Status: **superseded by ADR-0204** (2026-09-28: the user chose the free APIs; source Alpaca SIP 2016+, `dataGrade: "free-2016"`, explicit tail compensation). The measurements below stay valid · Role: QE · Date: 2026-09-28 · Guide §1.2, §10.6 step 1, R-26

## Context
The calibration needs 20+ years of daily official open and close, splits and dividends for NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, MSFT, SPY and COIN (since its 2021 listing), plus comparable names for pooling, under a licence that allows **internal model use**. The outputs published on-chain are standardised gap sets (int16 thousandths of σ) and σ values, not prices.

## What the available keys give (measured 2026-09-28)
| Vendor / plan | History | Limits | Licence | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Polygon/Massive **Basic** (in `.env`) | 2 years (`NOT_AUTHORIZED` before) | 5 req/min | individual, non-professional | cross-check only |
| Alpaca **Basic** (in `.env`) | SIP daily bars from **2016-01-04**; corporate actions from 2016 | 200 req/min | personal use | dev data only |
| Massive Stocks Advanced | 20+ years | unlimited | individual only | not internal-business |
| **Massive Stocks Business** | 20+ years | unlimited | business | $2,499/month |
| **Tiingo Power, business licence** | 30+ years EOD (open, high, low, close, `splitFactor`, `divCash`) | 10k req/hour | internal business use, no redistribution | **$50/month, recommended** |

Alpaca's daily-bar open/close are the consolidated official prints: on a sample of dates they equal Polygon's `/v1/open-close` official open and close to the cent (checked 2025-06-02 NVDA: 135.49 / 137.38 on both).

## Decision
1. **Recommended licensed source: Tiingo Power (business), $50/month.** The adapter exists (`credence_cal/vendors.py::Tiingo`); `make cal-data VENDOR=tiingo` pulls 2000→ and `make cal-all VENDOR=tiingo` rebuilds everything.
2. Until the user buys it, the pipeline runs on **Alpaca SIP 2016-01-04 → 2026-09-25** (10.7 years, 37 symbols). Every output carries `dataGrade: "dev-unlicensed"`, and the proposal is marked DRAFT. It must not be executed on a public network.
3. **Pinning.** Raw Parquet lives in `calibration/data/raw/<vendor>/` (git-ignored). The committed manifest `calibration/manifests/data-<vendor>.json` records vendor, licence grade, pull time, window, and per symbol the rows, first/last date, split and dividend counts, `contentSha256` (sha256 of a canonical CSV of the rows, independent of the Parquet writer version) and `fileSha256`. Every pipeline run first verifies the raw files against the manifest (`make cal-verify`) and refuses to run on a mismatch.
4. CI cannot hold vendor data, so it runs the pipeline on a **synthetic** sample generated by committed code (`credence_cal/sample.py`), and checks its output hashes (`out-sample/HASHES.sample.json`).

## Consequences
- 10.7 years instead of 20+: roughly 480 weekends and 100 holiday closures per name, so pooling across comparables carries more weight (ADR-0203), and the backtest cannot include 2000–2015 (the dot-com unwind, 2008). The report flags this.
- A licensed re-pull changes every hash; that is expected and is exactly what the proposal diff shows.

# ADR-0003 · Calendar windows the guide leaves open

Status: accepted · Role: BE-backend · Date: 2026-09-27 · Guide §8.2.1, §10.6 step 8

1. **Early-close days (13:00 ET): post-market ends at 17:00 ET, not 20:00.** The guide's table gives 16:00 → 20:00 for post-market and is silent on early closes. On early-close days US venues run extended trading only until 17:00, so the `extClose` of such a session is 17:00. Early closes are always followed by a non-session day (Black Friday, Christmas Eve, the day before Independence Day), so the next session's `extOpen` (20:00 ET the evening before it) never overlaps.
2. **USBANK sessions are `extOpen 08:00, open 09:00, close 17:00, extClose 18:00` ET** on Federal Reserve business days. `CalendarStore` requires strictly increasing times inside a session (§8.2.1), and a fund has no extended window. The regular window 09:00–17:00 matches the 17:00 ET NAV strike (§10.1). The 1-hour buffers are nominal and change no NAV valuation (NAV markets value at NAV in every state, §8.3.2).
3. **Fed holiday rules, not NYSE rules, for USBANK:** a Sunday holiday is observed on Monday. A **Saturday holiday is not moved to Friday** (Fed banks are open that Friday), unlike `pandas.USFederalHolidayCalendar`. Columbus Day and Veterans Day are bank holidays.
4. **24/5 overnight:** every XNYS session's `extOpen` is 20:00 ET on the previous calendar day. Sessions are Mon–Fri, so this is the "Sun–Thu 20:00" rule, and it also opens overnight trading after a mid-week holiday (Thanksgiving Thursday 20:00 → Black Friday).
5. **Closure classification:** OVERNIGHT if the next session is the next calendar day. WEEKEND if only Saturday/Sunday lie in between. Otherwise HOLIDAY_WEEKEND, which includes mid-week holidays (Types.sol comment).
6. **File format:** one JSON object per venue, `<VENUE>-<from>-<to>.json` (dates as YYYYMMDD). `.sessions` is the `Session[]` in struct field order, and `.sessionsAbiEncoded` is `abi.encode(Session[])`, because Foundry's `vm.parseJson` decodes object keys alphabetically. It also carries `contentHash` (sha256 of the compact sessions) and `sessionDates`. CI regenerates the committed files and fails on any diff.

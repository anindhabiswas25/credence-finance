# ADR-0006 · Keeper execution model: fencing, write-ahead transactions, J1 coalescing

Status: accepted · Role: BE-backend · Date: 2026-09-27 · Guide §10.2, §11.3

1. **Leader election.** The leader holds `pg_try_advisory_lock(fnv64("credence-keeper:<chainId>"))` on a **dedicated connection** and heartbeats `ops.keeper_instance` through it every second. **All** job-store writes and transaction write-aheads use that connection, so an instance that lost the lock cannot record or send anything (fencing).
   - A crashed leader releases the lock with its connection, and the follower takes over on its next attempt (≈ 1 s; the test asserts < 15 s).
   - A hung leader that keeps its connection is fenced: when its heartbeat is older than **15 s**, the follower `pg_terminate_backend`s the lock-holding backend (found in `pg_locks` for this database) and takes over (the test asserts 14–20 s).
2. **Write-ahead transactions.** Each transaction is signed locally (nonce, gas = estimate × 1.3, EIP-1559 fees), its hash is inserted into `ops.keeper_tx` (`pending`) and the job marked `submitted`, **then** it is broadcast. A new leader that finds a `submitted` job reconciles its recorded hashes (mined → done; in mempool → wait; gone → `dropped`, and the job may run again). It never blindly re-sends. The test crashes a leader between broadcast and marking, and asserts exactly one tx for that key.
3. **Nonce manager.** A single sender per chain; the local counter syncs from the pending nonce on becoming leader and on any nonce error. **Stuck transactions** are replaced after 3 blocks at the same nonce with fees +20%. All hashes are written ahead, and the first mined one wins; the rest are marked `replaced`.
4. **Two RPC providers** with failover on every call (`keeper_rpc_failovers_total`). Cross-checking reads against the second provider during REOPEN arrives with J5 (S3).
5. **J1 coalescing.** Idempotency keys are `J1:<asset>:<boundary>` for every calendar boundary (extOpen, open, bellWindow = close − 2 h, bell = close − 15 min, close, extClose), fired at boundary + 1 s, plus `J1:<asset>:hb:<minute>` every 60 s. When several keys of one asset are due in one tick (e.g. after a restart within the 15-minute lookback), **one** `poke` (lazy, idempotent on-chain) covers them all: the newest boundary key sends, and the others are recorded `done` with `coveredBy`. One tx per asset per trigger, never one per missed key.
6. **J12 housekeeping** runs once per UTC day per check (`J12:<check>:<date>`). Calendar coverage is read on-chain (`CalendarStore.coverageEnd(bytes32(venue))`, alert < 30 days). Wallet balances cover the keeper sender plus `WATCH_WALLETS` (alert below `KEEPER_MIN_BALANCE_WEI`). The Stylus `programTimeLeft` check is recorded `skipped` until the Risk Engine is deployed (S2). Alerts go to `ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL` and `keeper_alerts_total`.
